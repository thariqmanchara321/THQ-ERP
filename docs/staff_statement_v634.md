# Complete Staff statement v6.3.4

Open a Staff member to view the complete selected-period statement: Staff ID, opening balance, earnings, payments, chronological running balances and closing balances. Closing amounts due and available advances are shown separately by store, so a different store's advance cannot conceal a due. Current balances are labelled separately and can include later transactions. Empty periods carry the opening balance forward.

The statement includes profile, dated payslips, payment allocations, salary allocation to loads and activity history. Payslip paid/outstanding and remaining advances are calculated through the selected end date; later payments or advance applications do not change those dated figures. Salary allocations to loads remain informational and are not counted as a second payroll expense. No transaction or detail-row limit is imposed. Attendance remains reserved for a future independent module.

Print, PDF and Excel use the same complete statement response. PDF uses bundled fonts, paginates all rows and long details, and keeps section headings with their first detail. Excel preserves numeric money and splits long saved text into numbered parts. Both formats include Staff ID and meaningful document/load codes. Shared saved-record previews and human report exports omit internal UUIDs and technical reference keys recursively, without mutating saved data or transaction requests. Complete JSON exports retain underlying identifiers for recovery and integration.
