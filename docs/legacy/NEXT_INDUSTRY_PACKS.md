# Next industry packs

The platform foundation is ready, but these workflows should be implemented as dedicated packs, not generic placeholders.

## Restaurant pack
Tables/sections, menu modifiers, dine-in/takeaway/delivery orders, KOT/kitchen display, split bills, tips/service charge, reservations, recipe/ingredient consumption, waiter permissions.
Completed restaurant bills should post through the standard Sales engine and accounting.

## Workshop pack
Vehicle registry, odometer/history, job cards, inspection checklist/photos, estimates/approval, parts reservation/issue, labour/service lines, technician assignment, job status, invoice conversion, warranty/service reminders.

## Healthcare pack
Patient identity, appointments, doctor/resource schedules, clinical encounters, prescriptions, consent/privacy controls, strict clinical-vs-billing permissions and immutable access audit.
Do not expose clinical data to ordinary cashier/report roles.

## Diagnostic Lab pack
Test catalogue/panels, lab orders, sample/accession IDs, collection status, processing, result entry, verification, reference ranges, printable result reports, doctor/referrer linkage and strict result permissions.

## Pharmacy pack
Batch/lot, manufacture/expiry dates, MRP, purchase batch receipt, FEFO issue, near-expiry alerts, prescription linkage where required, returns and recall tracking.

## POS phase 2
Hold/resume cart, returns/refunds, receipt printing, barcode label printing, keyboard shortcuts, customer quick-create, promotions/price lists, split tender, cash drawer session/open-close, cashier shift summary, offline-safe queue only after conflict/reconciliation design.
