# THQ ERP — Operations Intelligence Architecture Specification

## 1. Executive Summary & Vision

THQ ERP Operations Intelligence transforms THQ ERP from an operational recording system into an advanced, automated **Business Intelligence and Decision Platform**.

### Key Architectural Invariants
1. **Deterministic & Algorithm-First:**
   All core intelligence engines, financial calculations, Pareto classifications, anomaly detections, predictive models, and business simulations run deterministically via rigorously validated mathematical algorithms. They require zero cloud AI subscriptions, external API tokens, or internet connectivity to function with 100% precision.
2. **Strictly Read-Only AI Assistance:**
   The optional conversational AI assistant operates under a strict read-only security boundary. It only receives sanitized, authorized summaries and is architecturally incapable of triggering database mutations, posting journal entries, or executing actions without explicit human intervention.
3. **Multi-Tenant & Location Isolation:**
   All insights, metrics, proposals, and database routines strictly enforce tenant isolation and location scoping using PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) policies.
4. **Governed Human-in-the-Loop Action Centre:**
   Automated proposals generated by intelligence engines require authorized human review, permission verification, and explicit approval before any transaction is executed.

## 3. Database Layer Foundation (v830)

### 3.1 Tables
- **`public.erp_intelligence_insights`**: Stores standardized insight records with tenant isolation, location scoping, severity classifications, category tags, confidence scores, and deduplication hashes.
- **`public.erp_intelligence_actions`**: Stores auditable proposals generated by intelligence engines or users. Maintains status progression (`pending_approval`, `approved`, `rejected`, `executed`, `failed`) and stores decision audit notes.

### 3.2 Canonical v830 Functions
1. **`operations_intelligence_summary_v830`**:
   Aggregates net sales, sales returns, gross profit, gross margin %, operating expenses, inventory value, monitored variant counts, stockouts, customer receivables, overdue debt, and supplier payables in a single server-side query.
2. **`inventory_intelligence_v830`**:
   Universal inventory intelligence covering all active variants. Computes average daily sales, days of cover, suggested reorder quantities, and performs dynamic **ABC Pareto classification** (Class A: top 80% revenue, Class B: next 15%, Class C: bottom 5%).
3. **`erp_intelligence_insight_status_v830`**:
   Updates status (`new_insight`, `acknowledged`, `in_progress`, `resolved`, `dismissed`, `reopened`) with resolution timestamps and optional resolution notes.
4. **`erp_intelligence_action_decide_v830`**:
   Atomically records approval or rejection of action proposals with decision timestamps and audit notes.

## 4. The 9 Business Intelligence Engines

| Engine | Primary Metric / Ratio | Key Intelligence Capability |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Executive Health** | Composite Index (0–100) | Multi-factor weighted score across Margin, Liquidity, Stock Health, and Pipeline friction. |
| **Sales Intelligence** | Sales Velocity & Run-rate | Identifies daily sales run rate, peak transaction hours, product revenue concentration, and elevated return rates. |
| **Inventory Intelligence** | ABC Pareto & Days Cover | Flags critical stockouts in Class A items, projects days of stock cover, and highlights dead stock capital lockup. |
| **Financial Intelligence** | Working Capital & CCC | Calculates Net Working Capital ($CA - CL$), Cash Conversion Cycle ($DIO + DSO - DPO$), and Operating Expense Ratio. |
| **Customer Credit** | Aging & Limit Utilization | Monitors overdue debt across aging buckets (1–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days) and flags credit limit breaches. |
| **Supplier Intelligence** | Payables Aging & Concentration | Tracks vendor debt aging, oldest due dates, and flags over-reliance on top suppliers. |
| **Procurement & Ops** | Economic Order Qty (EOQ) | Computes optimal reorder quantities $\sqrt{\frac{2DS}{H}}$ and tracks operational bottlenecks (draft GRNs, transit transfers). |
| **Operational Efficiency** | Friction & Sync Index | Monitors offline POS sync conflicts, unapproved POs, and pending purchase requests. |
| **Workforce Intelligence** | Cashier Velocity | Calculates cashier transactions per hour, average basket size, and item velocity. |

## 5. Risk & Anomaly Watchdog

The `RiskAnomalyEngine` scans transactional streams for operational risks:
- **Unusual Discounts:** Flags transactions where discount percentage exceeds 20% or an executive threshold.
- **Negative Margin Sales:** Identifies sales where selling price is below unit cost ($P < C$).
- **Duplicate Payment Detection:** Flags multiple payments made to the same vendor or party for identical amounts within a 7-day window.
- **Return Rate Spikes:** Detects products where sales return volume exceeds 10% of total volume sold.

---

## 6. Predictive Statistical Forecasting

The `PredictiveIntelligenceEngine` and `PredictiveModels` provide deterministic time-series forecasting:
- **Simple Moving Average (SMA):** Unweighted rolling baseline for stable demand profiles.
- **Exponential Moving Average (EMA):** Dynamically weights recent demand using smoothing factor $\alpha$.
- **Linear Regression Trend ($y = mx + b$):** Computes demand slope, intercept, and $R^2$ goodness-of-fit to project step-ahead demand.
- **Validation Accuracy:** Continuously evaluates Mean Absolute Error (MAE) and Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE).
- **Stockout Projection:** Calculates dynamic days remaining before inventory depletion based on rolling velocity.

## 7. Business Simulation & Decision Engine

The `BusinessSimulationEngine` allows executives to model what-if scenarios in memory without mutating live ledgers:
1. **Price Elasticity Simulator:**
   Adjusts selling price by $\Delta P\%$ and models volume impact using elasticity coefficient $E_d$:
   $$\% \Delta Q = E_d \cdot \% \Delta P$$
   Calculates projected net revenue, gross margin, and net profit delta.
2. **Bulk Purchase vs Holding Cost Simulator:**
   Evaluates vendor volume discounts against annual inventory carrying costs ($H = Q/2 \times C \times r$) over the estimated sell-through period.
3. **Credit Terms & DSO Simulator:**
   Calculates working capital released by shortening Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) and computes annual cost-of-capital savings.

---

## 8. Intelligence Action Centre

The `IntelligenceActionCentre` bridges analytics and operations:
- **Proposals Generated:** Purchase Order creation, Stock Transfers, Customer Payment Reminders, and Reorder Level adjustments.
- **Human Approval Workflow:** Actions remain in `pending_approval` until an authorized user reviews the verifiable evidence and explicitly approves or rejects the proposal.
- **One-Click PO Generation:** In the Procurement tab, users can multi-select suggested reorders and generate consolidated Draft Purchase Orders grouped by supplier.

---

## 9. Read-Only AI Business Assistant

The `ReadOnlyAiBusinessAssistant`:
- Exposes natural language Q&A for business performance ("What is our highest risk stock?", "Explain our CCC").
- Uses strictly sanitized context generated from live database snapshots.
- Operates under a 100% read-only interface; it has no database write credentials or mutation tools.
- Defaults to a hermetic deterministic provider (`MockAiIntelligenceProvider`) that functions completely offline without external cloud dependencies.
